## What went wrong, and whose fault it was

The model held the `## Section` format every time: no repair call in 40 runs.

1. **All 17 draft failures come from the role parser, not the model.** The Planner wrote role prompts containing a
   `{placeholder}`, for example `"Calculate the following expression: {expression}"`. The role parser is AutoAgents'
   regex `{[\s\S]*?}` (environment.py:62), copied as is. It is non-greedy, so it stops at the first `}` inside the
   prompt. The JSON is then invalid and the role is skipped. That leaves one role ("roster size 1") or none ("empty
   plan"). A brace-balanced parser (`parse_json_objects`, already used for capability requests) recovers 2 valid roles
   and a matching plan in all 17.
2. **Every flat answer is wrong because of a `---` fence.** The model wraps each reply in `---` lines, copied from the
   FORMAT_EXAMPLE. `parse_sections` keeps the closing `---` in the last section, so the Final Output `ActionInput`
   becomes `"EPOLEVNE\n---"`. With the fence stripped, 11/11 drafted flat answers are correct. boss_reviewers is not
   affected: the solver writes plain text.
3. **2 genuine model errors** (boss_reviewers): it reversed "envelope" as `EPOLOVNE`, twice.

Both 1 and 2 are cases where our copied parsing is stricter than the model's output. They are not prompt problems.
The fixes would be new deviations (proposed D22 and D23) and are not applied here.
