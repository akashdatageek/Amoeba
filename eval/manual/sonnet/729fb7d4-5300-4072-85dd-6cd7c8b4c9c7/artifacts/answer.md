### Caveats and unverified items
- **45W:** the cutoff is based on the date acquired, not the date placed in service. The IRS notes that acquiring before the cutoff does not by itself earn the credit; the vehicle must also be placed in service [S1]. A non-official source describes "acquired" as a binding written contract plus a payment [S5]. This matters only if the fleet already had a binding contract and payment by 30 Sep 2025 [S1], which would not be a 2026 purchase. I assumed none exists. If one does, up to $40,000 per truck [S2] would be an upside to check with a tax adviser.
- **30C:** the 30 Jun 2026 cutoff [S6] has passed. If any charger was already placed in service on or before that date, the credit could apply, subject to the eligible-census-tract test [S6]. I assumed none was.
- **VW trust:** the status shown comes from a webinar transcript snippet [S17] and a trust summary [S19]. I did not open the IDEM program page and did not retrieve an eligibility table, deadline or award percentage. Treat any amount as upside only. - **Source dates:** the source pages do not show their own publication dates in the excerpts I read. The "as-of" dates are the date I checked them, except where a source carries its own date (IDEM webinar, 21 Apr 2026 [S17]).

## Derived values (calculation shown)
- Midpoints: diesel (150,000 + 200,000) / 2 = 175,000; BEV (280,000 + 400,000) / 2 = 340,000; charger (80,000 + 250,000) / 2 = 165,000. Endpoints are from [S38] and [S43].
- Electricity rate in dollars: 13.93 ¢/kWh [S26] / 100 = $0.1393/kWh.
- BEV energy per mile at base: 1.5 kWh/mi [S36] × $0.1393/kWh = $0.2090/mi, about $0.209/mi.
- Diesel energy per mile at spot: $6.286/gal [S28] / 6.5 mpg [unverified] = $0.967/mi. The mpg is a placeholder, so this is provisional.
- Annual energy gap at spot diesel: ($0.967 − $0.209) × 60,000 mi (task) = $0.758 × 60,000 = $45,480, about $45,000 per truck per year.
- Year-ago diesel case: $3.731/gal [S32] / 6.5 mpg [unverified] = $0.574/mi. Gap = ($0.574 − $0.209) × 60,000 = $0.365 × 60,000 = $21,900 per truck per year. This corrects the earlier "about $20,000".
- The BEV figure assumes no extra demand-charge cost, no charging-efficiency loss and no off-peak pricing.
