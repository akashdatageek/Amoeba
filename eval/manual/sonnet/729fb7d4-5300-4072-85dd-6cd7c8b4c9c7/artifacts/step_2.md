## Derived values (calculation shown)
- Midpoints: diesel (150,000 + 200,000) / 2 = 175,000; BEV (280,000 + 400,000) / 2 = 340,000; charger (80,000 + 250,000) / 2 = 165,000. Endpoints are from [S38] and [S43].
- Electricity rate in dollars: 13.93 ¢/kWh [S26] / 100 = $0.1393/kWh.
- BEV energy per mile at base: 1.5 kWh/mi [S36] × $0.1393/kWh = $0.2090/mi, about $0.209/mi.
- Diesel energy per mile at spot: $6.286/gal [S28] / 6.5 mpg [unverified] = $0.967/mi. The mpg is a placeholder, so this is provisional.
- Annual energy gap at spot diesel: ($0.967 − $0.209) × 60,000 mi (task) = $0.758 × 60,000 = $45,480, about $45,000 per truck per year.
- Year-ago diesel case: $3.731/gal [S32] / 6.5 mpg [unverified] = $0.574/mi. Gap = ($0.574 − $0.209) × 60,000 = $0.365 × 60,000 = $21,900 per truck per year. This corrects the earlier "about $20,000".
- The BEV figure assumes no extra demand-charge cost, no charging-efficiency loss and no off-peak pricing.

## Gaps and source conflicts
1. **Diesel latest-week conflict.**
   - FRED/EIA shows Midwest diesel at **6.286** for 2026-10-05 [S28].
   - YCharts shows "current 6.526, down from 6.68 last week" [S32]. That appears to be the prior week, so YCharts is likely lagged.
   - The EIA Midwest page excerpt [S29] was truncated: "5.636 | 5.571 | 5.946 | 6.250 | 6.680 | 6.52…". I could not confirm which column is the latest week.
   - I chose 6.286 because it is the official EIA series with a stated date. Re-check on the EIA page before finalizing.
2. **No Indiana-specific diesel series** was retrieved. Midwest PADD 2 is used as the proxy, as the working interpretation allows.
3. **No 12-month averages** were obtained for diesel or electricity. The electricity base is a 7-month YTD value (13.93¢) [S26].
4. **Vehicle prices** come only from a secondary blog [S38], with unconfirmed column headers and no specific model, quote, battery size, range or warranty. The CARB draft [S35] is from 2020 and is not comparable. No OEM or dealer quote was found.
5. **Efficiency:** BEV kWh/mi rests on one older ORNL data point [S36]. No diesel mpg source and no payload-penalty numbers were found.
6. **Maintenance $/mi for both powertrains** has no sourced value. This blocks a sourced maintenance line in the TCO. The two provisional values are unsourced [unverified].
7. **Charger cost:** ranges come from contractor and vendor blogs [S42][S43][S44], not NREL or a utility quote. The NREL search returned no usable NREL cost page. Interconnection lead time and Indiana demand charges are missing.
8. **ICCT [S39] and NREL/NLR [S37]** were found (the ICCT piece uses a 250 mi/day, 62,500 mi/yr, 5-year case [S39]; the NLR paper cites breakeven charging costs of $0.17–$0.38/kWh for high-power Class 8 charging [S37]), but no extractable price, efficiency or maintenance figures were read from them. They are worth fetching in a later pass.
9. **Cross-check on US commercial electricity:** secondary sites report the US average commercial price as 13.51¢ for Apr 2026 [S25], 14.19¢ for Jun 2026 [S22] and 13.64¢ for Jan 2026 [S23]. These are the US average, not Indiana, and I did not use them for the base.
