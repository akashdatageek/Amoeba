# Commercial Clean Vehicle Credit

### More In Credits & Deductions

**Update:** The Qualified Commercial Clean Vehicle Credit is not available for [vehicles acquired](/newsroom/faqs-for-modification-of-sections-25c-25d-25e-30c-30d-45l-45w-and-179d-under-public-law-119-21-139-stat-72-july-4-2025-commonly-known-as-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-obbb "FAQs for modification of sections 25C, 25D, 25E, 30C, 30D, 45L, 45W, AND 179D under Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (July 4, 2025), commonly known as the One, Big, Beautiful Bill (OBBB)") after Sept. 30, 2025.

The vehicle must be placed in service for the taxpayer to claim the credit. If a vehicle is placed in service after Sept. 30, 2025, the taxpayer must have acquired the vehicle on or before Sept. 30, 2025, to be eligible for the credit. A taxpayer can demonstrate acquisition by entering into a binding written contract and making a payment on the vehicle on or before Sept. 30, 2025. A taxpayer places the vehicle in service when they take possession of the vehicle.

This page covers changes under the [Working Families Tax Cuts](/newsroom/working-families-tax-cuts "Working Families Tax Cuts").

Businesses and tax-exempt organizations that place in service a qualified commercial clean vehicle may qualify for a clean vehicle tax credit of up to $40,000 under Internal Revenue Code (IRC) 45W. No credit is available for vehicles [acquired](/newsroom/faqs-for-modification-of-sections-25c-25d-25e-30c-30d-45l-45w-and-179d-under-public-law-119-21-139-stat-72-july-4-2025-commonly-known-as-the-one-big-beautiful-bill-obbb "FAQs for modification of sections 25C, 25D, 25E, 30C, 30D, 45L, 45W, AND 179D under Public Law 119-21, 139 Stat. 72 (July 4, 2025), commonly known as the One, Big, Beautiful Bill (OBBB)") after Sept. 30, 2025.

## Credit amount

Follow these steps to calculate the credit amount:

1. Find the maximum credit amount based on the gross vehicle weight rating (GVWR) of the vehicle:

2. Calculate a percentage of [your basis](/forms-pubs/about-publication-551 "About Publication 551, Basis of Assets") (the amount of your investment for tax purposes) in the vehicle based on engine type:

3. Find the incremental cost of the vehicle (the excess of its purchase price over that of a comparable vehicle powered only by gas or diesel internal combustion).

For electric vehicles [placed in service in 2024](/newsroom/irs-issues-guidance-on-the-incremental-cost-for-the-commercial-clean-vehicle-credit "IRS issues guidance on the incremental cost for the Commercial Clean Vehicle Credit"), use the Department of Energy’s incremental cost analysis for the appropriate class of vehicle:

For previous calendar years, please refer to the Department of Energy's [incremental cost methodology page](https://www.energy.gov/eere/vehicles/incremental-purchase-cost-methodology-and-results-electric-vehicles "Incremental purchase cost methodology and results for electric vehicles").

4. Compare the maximum credit amount, percentage of basis, and incremental cost. The **smallest figure** is your credit amount.

## Who qualifies

Businesses and tax-exempt organizations qualify for the credit.

There is no limit on the number of credits your business can claim. For businesses, the credits are nonrefundable, so you can't get back more on the credit than you owe in taxes. A 45W credit can be carried over as a general business credit.

## How to claim the credit

Partnerships and S corporations must file [Form 8936, Clean Vehicle Credits](/forms-pubs/about-form-8936 "About Form 8936, Clean Vehicle Credit").

All other taxpayers report this credit on line 1y in Part III of [Form 3800, General Business Credit](/forms-pubs/about-form-3800 "About Form 3800, General Business Credit").

A tax-exempt entity must file a [Form 990-T, Exempt Organization Business Income Tax Return PDF](/pub/irs-pdf/f990t.pdf "2025 Form 990-T                          (PDF)") with an attached Form 3800, even if a Form 990-T would not otherwise be required to be filed.
