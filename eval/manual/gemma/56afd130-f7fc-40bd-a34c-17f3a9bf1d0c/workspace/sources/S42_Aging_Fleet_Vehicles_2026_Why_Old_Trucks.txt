## Warning Signs: When to Replace

Beyond cost-per-mile, watch for indicators a vehicle has become a liability:

#### Major Component Failures

Repairs exceeding 40% of vehicle value signal replacement time.

#### Chronic Downtime

72+ hours unplanned downtime per year indicates unreliable asset.

#### Safety Concerns

Repeated brake, steering, or suspension issues create liability exposure.

#### Driver Complaints

When drivers refuse assignments to specific vehicles, retention suffers.
