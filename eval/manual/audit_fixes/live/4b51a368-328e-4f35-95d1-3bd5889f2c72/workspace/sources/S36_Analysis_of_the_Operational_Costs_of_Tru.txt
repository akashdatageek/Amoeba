# Analysis of the Operational Costs of Trucking: 2026 Update

This report presents the results of a survey of for-hire U.S. and Canadian motor carriers on 2025 operational costs. The survey, conducted during February 2026-May 2026, included 182,248 combination truck-tractors. Data includes: regional costs, fleet size, driver compensation, fuel costs, equipment costs, truck insurance premiums, and other marginal costs. Performance indicator data includes: truck driver turnover, fuel economy, and dwell time. Overall the average cost per mile for motor carriers in 2025 was $2.336 per mile, a 3.4% increase from 2024's $2.26 per mile. Fuel costs in 2025 were 48.2 cents per mile as compared to 2024's 48.1 cents per mile. Lease or purchase payments for trucks and trailers increased to 40.4 cents per mile as compared to 2024's 39 cents per mile; insurance premiums increased by 3.9%; and repair and maintenance costs increased by 8.6%. The average driver wage per-mile increased to 81.8 cents per mile in 2025, a 2.5% increase from 2024's 79.8 cents per mile.
