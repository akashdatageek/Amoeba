A regional freight carrier based in Indianapolis, Indiana runs 35 Class 8 tractors that average 6.2 miles per gallon and about 2,300 miles per week each. It wants a fuel-surcharge schedule for its shippers and a picture of its fuel cost over the past year.

2. For each week, compute the fuel surcharge per mile with the standard method, (weekly price − $1.25 base) ÷ 6.2 mpg, rounded to the cent, and the fleet's total fuel cost for that week.
3. Find the latest American Transportation Research Institute (ATRI) average marginal cost per mile for trucking and the share of it that is fuel, and compare it with the carrier's average fuel cost per mile over the 52 weeks.
4. Deliver an Excel workbook (weekly price, surcharge and fleet fuel cost, with formulas), a PNG line chart of price and surcharge over the 52 weeks, and a Word memo of at most 2 pages. The memo gives the surcharge for each 5-cent price band from $3.00 to $5.00, the average, highest and lowest week, the ATRI comparison, and the source and date of every figure. State any figure you could not get and what you used instead.
