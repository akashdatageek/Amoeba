### Recommendation

**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Fleet Management
**FROM:** TCO Modeler
**DATE:** October 11, 2026
**SUBJECT:** Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) Analysis: Diesel vs. Battery-Electric Tractors

**Executive Summary**
Based on a 7-year total cost of ownership analysis, it is recommended that the carrier **do not** replace the current diesel fleet with battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) at this time. The BEV option results in a total cost increase of **$166,200 per truck** ($640,200 - $474,000) over the 7-year horizon.

**Financial Analysis**
The transition to BEVs offers significant operational savings in energy and maintenance. Specifically, BEVs reduce annual operating expenses by approximately **$23,400 per truck** compared to diesel, calculated as: (($231,000 + $63,000) - ($92,400 + $37,800)) / 7 years. However, these savings are heavily outweighed by the high initial capital requirements:
*   **Purchase Price Premium:** Even after applying the $40,000 [unverified] Federal 45W credit, the BEV purchase price remains $180,000 higher than the diesel equivalent ($360,000 - $180,000).
*   **Infrastructure Burden:** The required charging infrastructure adds a substantial $150,000 [unverified] per truck to the initial investment.

**Break-Even Logic**
The total upfront premium for switching to BEV (Net Purchase Price + Infrastructure minus Diesel Purchase Price) is **$330,000 per truck** ($360,000 + $150,000 - $180,000). Given the annual operational savings of $23,400, the break-even point for the investment is approximately **14.1 years** ($330,000 / $23,400). This far exceeds the 7-year analysis window and the typical useful life of the tractor assets.

**Assumptions**
*   **Annual Mileage:** 60,000 miles per truck.
*   **Horizon:** 7 years.
*   **Residual Value:** No salvage value was attributed to either vehicle type.
*   **Price Stability:** Fuel and electricity rates are assumed to remain constant at October 2026 levels ($3.85/gal diesel [unverified]; $0.11/kWh electricity [unverified]).
*   **Maintenance:** Costs are based on industry benchmarks of $0.15/mi for diesel and $0.09/mi for BEV [unverified].
