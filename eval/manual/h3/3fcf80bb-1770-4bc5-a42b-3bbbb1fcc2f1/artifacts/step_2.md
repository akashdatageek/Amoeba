### Charger Selection Logic
For a fleet of 20 Class 8 tractors with a daily range of <250 miles, **150kW DC Fast Chargers (DCFC)** are selected. According to the *EV Fleet Charging Infrastructure Guide 2026* [S89], a 150kW DCFC delivers 150–400 miles of range per hour depending on the vehicle's acceptance rate. This ensures that regional tractors can be fully recharged within standard overnight dwell times or driver rest periods, providing the necessary operational reliability for the specified routes.
