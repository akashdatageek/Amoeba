### Charger Selection Logic
For a fleet of 20 Class 8 tractors with a daily range of <250 miles, **150kW DC Fast Chargers (DCFC)** are selected. Class 8 trucks typically utilize batteries in the 300–600 kWh range, making DCFC the operational standard to ensure vehicles can be recharged within overnight windows or during driver rest periods [S11]. 150kW is the industry benchmark for regional depot charging in 2026 [S11, S13, S19], providing the necessary balance between charging speed and grid capacity.
