### Charging Infrastructure Cost Estimate

**Charger Selection Logic:**
For a fleet of 20 Class 8 tractors with a daily range of <250 miles, 150kW DC Fast Chargers (DCFC) are selected. Class 8 trucks typically utilize batteries in the 300–600 kWh range, making DCFC the operational standard [S11]. A 150kW output is sufficient to recharge the vehicles during overnight depot stays or driver rest periods, ensuring operational readiness for regional routes without requiring ultra-fast (350kW+) hardware, which would significantly increase costs [S11, S16].

**Infrastructure Cost Breakdown**

| Component | Cost per Unit | Total Cost | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| 150kW DC Fast Charger | $75,000 [S10, S16] | $1,500,000 (20 * $75,000) | Hardware cost for 150kW DCFC units [S10, S16] |
| Installation | $45,000 [S10] | $900,000 (20 * $45,000) | Labor, trenching, and wiring [S10] |
| Utility Upgrades | $30,000 [S10] | $600,000 (20 * $30,000) | Transformers and switchgear for 3MW load [S10] |
| Permits & Soft Costs | $3,000 [S10] | $60,000 (20 * $3,000) | Permitting and commissioning [S10] |
| **Total** | **$153,000** (see below) | **$3,060,000** (see below) | **Total CAPEX for 20-truck depot** |

**Calculations:**
*   **Total Infrastructure Cost:** $1,500,000 + $900,000 + $600,000 + $60,000 = $3,060,000
*   **Cost per Truck:** $3,060,000 / 20 = $153,000

**Summary Totals:**
*   **Total Infrastructure Cost:** $3,060,000
*   **Cost per Truck:** $153,000
