**MEMORANDUM: Machine Acquisition Cost Analysis**

* **Total Lease Cost**
Formula: Monthly Payment * Number of Months
Calculation: $1,150 * 36 = $41,400

* **Net Buy Cost**
Formula: Purchase Price - Resale Value
Calculation: $38,500 - $6,000 = $32,500

* **Cost Difference**
Formula: Total Lease Cost - Net Buy Cost
Calculation: $41,400 - $32,500 = $8,900

**Conclusion:**
Buying the machine is $8,900 cheaper than leasing it over the 36-month period (Calculation: $41,400 - $32,500 = $8,900).
