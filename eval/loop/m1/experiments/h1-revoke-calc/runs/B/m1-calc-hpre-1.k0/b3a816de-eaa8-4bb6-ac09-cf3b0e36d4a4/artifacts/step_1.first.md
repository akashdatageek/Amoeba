# Loan Amortization Calculation
## Calculation Parameters
- Formula used: M = P [ i(1 + i)^n ] / [ (1 + i)^n – 1 ]
- Principal (P): 9,500
- Annual Interest Rate: 7.2%
- Monthly Interest Rate (i): 0.072 / 12 = 0.006
- Number of Payments (n): 36

## Calculation Results
- Raw Monthly Payment: 9500 * [ 0.006 * (1.006)^36 ] / [ (1.006)^36 - 1 ] = 294.278
- Rounded Monthly Payment: 294.28 (rounded from 294.278)
- Total Interest: (294.28 * 36) - 9500 = 1,094.08
