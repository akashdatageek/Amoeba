# Flooring Material Estimation
## Material Requirements
1. Floor Area: 7.2 m * 4.5 m = 32.4 m2
2. Tile Area: 0.6 m * 0.6 m = 0.36 m2
3. Exact Tiles: 32.4 m2 / 0.36 m2 = 90
4. Total Tiles (with 10% waste): 90 * (1 + 0.10) = 99
5. Boxes Required: 99 / 8 = 12.375, rounded up to 13 boxes

## Cost Analysis
6. Total Cost: 13 boxes * $42.50/box = $552.50
