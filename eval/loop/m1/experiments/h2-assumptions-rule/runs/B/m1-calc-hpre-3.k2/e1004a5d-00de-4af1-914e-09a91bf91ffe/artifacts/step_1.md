**CVP Analysis Memo**

* **Contribution Margin per Cake**
Formula: Selling Price - Variable Cost
Calculation: $12 - $7.50 = $4.5
Result: $4.5 per cake

* **Break-Even Quantity (BEQ)**
Formula: Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin
Calculation: $18,000 / $4.5 = 4,000
Result: 4,000 cakes

* **Break-Even Revenue (BER)**
Formula: Break-Even Quantity * Selling Price
Calculation: 4,000 * $12 = $48,000
Result: $48,000

* **Target Profit Quantity (TPQ)**
Formula: (Fixed Costs + Target Profit) / Contribution Margin
Calculation: ($18,000 + $9,000) / $4.5 = 6,000
Result: 6,000 cakes
