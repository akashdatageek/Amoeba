**MEMO: Calculation Sheet**

- **Contribution Margin per Cake**
Formula: Price - Variable Cost
Calculation: $12 - $7.50 = $4.50

- **Break-even Quantity (BEQ)**
Formula: Fixed Costs / Contribution Margin
Calculation: $18,000 / $4.50 = 4,000 cakes

- **Break-even Revenue (BER)**
Formula: Break-even Quantity * Price
Calculation: 4,000 * $12 = $48,000

- **Target Profit Quantity (TPQ)**
Formula: (Fixed Costs + Target Profit) / Contribution Margin
Calculation: ($18,000 + $9,000) / $4.50 = 6,000 cakes
