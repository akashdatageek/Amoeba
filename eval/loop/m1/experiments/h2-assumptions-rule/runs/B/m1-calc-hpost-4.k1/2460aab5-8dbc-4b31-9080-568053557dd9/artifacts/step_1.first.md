**MEMO: Annual Commuting Logistics**

**Step-by-Step Calculations:**
1. **Distance per day:** 22 km * 2 = 44 km
2. **Distance per week:** 44 km * 5 = 220 km
3. **Distance per year:** 220 km * 46 = 10,120 km
4. **Fuel per year:** (10,120 km / 100) * 7.8 L = 789.36 L
5. **Cost per year:** 789.36 L * $1.65/L = $1,302.44

**Annual Metrics:**
- Total Annual Distance: 10,120 km (220 km * 46)
- Total Annual Fuel Used: 789.36 L ((10,120 km / 100) * 7.8 L)
- Total Annual Fuel Cost: $1,302.44 (789.36 L * $1.65/L)
