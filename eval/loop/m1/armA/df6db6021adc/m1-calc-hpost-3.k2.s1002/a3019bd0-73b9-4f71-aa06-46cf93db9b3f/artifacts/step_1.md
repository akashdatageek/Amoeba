**Investment Calculation Memo**

**Investment Parameters:**
- Principal ($P$): $12,000
- Annual Interest Rate ($r$): 5.5% or 0.055
- Compounding Frequency ($n$): Quarterly (4 times per year)
- Time ($t$): 7 years

**Formula:**
$A = P(1 + r/n)^{nt}$

**Step-by-Step Calculations:**
1. **Periodic Interest Rate ($r/n$):**
   $0.055 / 4 = 0.01375$

2. **Total Number of Periods ($nt$):**
   $4 \times 7 = 28$

3. **Growth Factor $(1 + r/n)^{nt}$:**
   $(1 + 0.01375)^{28} = 1.4657647802100227$ [S1]

4. **Final Investment Value ($A$):**
   $12,000 \times 1.4657647802100227 = 17,589.17736...$
   Rounding to two decimal places: $A = 17,589.18$

5. **Total Interest Earned ($I$):**
   $17,589.18 - 12,000 = 5,589.18$

**Final Results:**
- Final Investment Value: **$17,589.18**
- Total Interest Earned: **$5,589.18**
