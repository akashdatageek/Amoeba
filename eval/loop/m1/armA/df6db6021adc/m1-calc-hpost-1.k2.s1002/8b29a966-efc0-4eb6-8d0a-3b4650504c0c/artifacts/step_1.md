# Memo: Lease vs. Buy Cost Analysis

## Executive Summary
This analysis compares the total cost of leasing a machine versus purchasing it over a 36-month period, ignoring interest and the time value of money.

## Calculations
- Total Lease Cost: 1,150 * 36 = 41,400
- Net Buy Cost: 38,500 - 6,000 = 32,500
- Savings: 41,400 - 32,500 = 8,900
