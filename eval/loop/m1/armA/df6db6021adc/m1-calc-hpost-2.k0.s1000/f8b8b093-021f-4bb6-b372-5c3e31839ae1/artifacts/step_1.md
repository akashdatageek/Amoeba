## Memo: Material Take-off
1. **Floor Area**: 7.2 m * 4.5 m = 32.4 m²
2. **Tile Area**: 0.6 m * 0.6 m = 0.36 m²
3. **Exact Tile Count**: 32.4 m² / 0.36 m² = 90 tiles
4. **Buffered Tile Count**: 90 tiles * 1.10 (10% buffer) = 99 tiles
5. **Boxes Required**: 99 tiles / 8 tiles per box = 12.375 boxes; rounded up to 13 boxes
6. **Total Procurement Cost**: 13 boxes * $42.50 = $552.50
