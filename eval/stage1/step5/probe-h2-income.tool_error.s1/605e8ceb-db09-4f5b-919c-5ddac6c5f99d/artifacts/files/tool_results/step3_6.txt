# 2020 ACS 1-Year Experimental Data Tables

Access the tables for the 2020 ACS 1-year experimental data release by clicking on the table titles below. These 54 tables cover social, economic, housing, and demographic characteristics, similar to the format of the ACS 1-year Supplemental Estimates.  You can also view the materials under Supporting Documentation for additional information.
