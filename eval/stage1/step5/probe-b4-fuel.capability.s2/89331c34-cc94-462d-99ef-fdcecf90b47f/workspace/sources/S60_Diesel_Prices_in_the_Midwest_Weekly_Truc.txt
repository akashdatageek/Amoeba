## Why there is no single-state price here

EIA publishes weekly diesel averages for the country, its regions and California, and no other state. A truck fueling anywhere in Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee or Wisconsin is inside this regional figure.

## Method: how each number was obtained

* The weekly prices are EIA's PADD 2on-highway diesel series, all types, in dollars a gallon, read from EIA's history table each day so that revisions carry over.
* EIA collects the cash self-serve price, taxes included, as of 8:00 a.m. local time each Monday.
* Federal and state diesel taxes and fees come from EIA's federal and state motor fuels tax table.
* Changes, highs and lows, gaps to the U.S. average, rank, surcharge figures and prices before taxes are our arithmetic on those figures.

The page updates only when EIA's figures change. Pages across the site follow[our rating methodology](/how-we-rate).

## What we could not verify

* County and local fuel taxes, and state taxes on gross or net receipts, which EIA's tax table leaves out.
* The price at any single truck stop in the Midwest, which can sit well away from the regional average.
* The base price and fuel economy in your own surcharge program.

## Sample, window and limitations

The Midwestseries starts on 21 March 1994. EIA changed its survey sample on 13 June 2022 and says prices from that week on are not directly comparable with earlier weeks. The chart shows two years and the table one.The prices before taxes use this week's regional price with each state's taxes, so each is an estimate: EIA measured no price for the state itself.

## Frequently asked questions

**What is the price of diesel in the Midwest this week?**

EIA's average retail on-highway diesel price for the Midwest was $6.259 a gallon for the week of 12 October 2026, down 2.7 cents on the week before and above the U.S. average by 8 cents.

**Which states are in the Midwest for diesel prices?**

EIA's the Midwest figure covers Illinois, Indiana, Iowa, Kansas, Kentucky, Michigan, Minnesota, Missouri, Nebraska, North Dakota, Ohio, Oklahoma, South Dakota, Tennessee and Wisconsin.

**How much is the fuel surcharge in the Midwest?**

It depends on your contract. With the Midwest's price of $6.259, a $1.250 base price and 7.28 miles a gallon, the surcharge comes to $0.688 a mile. A contract that reads the U.S. average uses $6.179 instead, so check which series yours names.

**What are diesel taxes in the Midwest?**

The Midwest's state diesel taxes and fees run from $0.200 a gallon in Oklahoma to $0.792 in Illinois in EIA's latest table, on top of the $0.244 federal tax.
