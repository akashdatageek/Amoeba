### 2020 Population, Race and Housing Data Extracts

Local data from the redistricting file (P.L. 94-171) for Indiana were released by the U.S. Census Bureau on August 12, 2021. We have extracted key variables from that file to enable easier use.

* [***IBRC Analysis*****:** Indiana’s Census 2020 results: Metro areas and minority populations fuel state’s growth](https://blog.kelley.iu.edu/2021/08/12/indianas-census-2020-results-metro-areas-and-minority-populations-fuel-states-growth/)
* [Indiana's Census 2020 Redistricting Data Dashboard](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2020/pl94/redistricting-data-dashboard.asp)

#### Excel Files

***Geographies:*** county, city, township, congressional district, state legislative (house and senate) districts, school districts and census tracts. ***Variables:*** total population; 18 and older; under age 18; white, black, Asian, other, and multi-race; Hispanic; white, non-Hispanic; a minority tabulation; total count of housing units, as well as the number that were occupied and the number vacant.

## Census 2000

* **[Detailed 60-page Socio-Economic Profiles for Indiana Counties (SF3)](../sf3profiles.asp)** Based on the content of Summary File 3, these provide a comprehensive set of data results based on the long form. These are in PDF format.
* **[Geographic Comparison Tables with 1990 and 2000 Data](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/c2kframe.html)** More than 30 tables with ranks and percent changes from 1990. All Indiana counties, cities, towns and townships. Use the selection options on the top frame to pick tables and select your geography.
* **[Four-Page Demographic Overview Profiles (DPs1-4) and Race/Ethnicity Profiles for Counties, Cities, Town and Townships](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/c2kframe2.html)**  
   Four-page demographic profiles (in PDF format) and a one-page race/ethnicity profile (in text format) for each county, city, town and township.
  + **Want to crunch all the numbers in these profiles?** Download the ASCII format of the four-page profile above. Save these files to your computer and load into a spreadsheet or database. Use the four-page profile as your guide to the content because each file is based on one of the four pages in the profile:   
     [Page 1 ![(Excel)](../images/icons/excel16.png)](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/indiana_dp1.csv) | [Page 2 ![(Excel)](../images/icons/excel16.png)](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/indiana_dp2.csv) | [Page 3 ![(Excel)](../images/icons/excel16.png)](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/indiana_dp3.csv) | [Page 4 ![(Excel)](../images/icons/excel16.png)](https://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/indiana_dp4.csv)
* [![map](/images/tract_icon.gif)**Fast Facts for Tracts**](http://www.stats.indiana.edu/c2k/fftr_frame.html) Twenty-five of the most often requested items plus thematic tract maps for each county, currently based on the 100 percent count data.

## Special Censuses

Local officials frequently request a Special Census—an enumeration of population, housing units and group quarters— when there has been a significant population change in their community due to annexation or growth.

* [Special Census Certified Counts for Governmental Units in Indiana](https://www.census.gov/programs-surveys/specialcensus/data_products/official_counts/indiana.html) (U.S. Census Bureau)
