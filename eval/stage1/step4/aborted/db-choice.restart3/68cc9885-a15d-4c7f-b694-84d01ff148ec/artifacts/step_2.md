- **No Atlas benchmark found.** I found no published third-party MongoDB Atlas benchmark. No source I read compares a managed PostgreSQL with Atlas.
- **No Aurora PostgreSQL benchmark found.** I found no Aurora PostgreSQL result (TPC-H, TPC-DS or similar).

- **Workload mismatch.** None tests a shared multi-tenant dashboard workload at 10 TB (50 × 200 GB) with 5,000 queries per minute combined.
