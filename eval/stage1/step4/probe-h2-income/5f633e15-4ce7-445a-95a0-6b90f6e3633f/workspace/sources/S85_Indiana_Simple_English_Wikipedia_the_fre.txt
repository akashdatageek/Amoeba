**Indiana** is a [U.S. state](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/U.S._state "U.S. state") in the [midwestern](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Midwestern_United_States "Midwestern United States") and [Great Lakes](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes "Great Lakes") regions of North America. Its [capital](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Capital_(city) "Capital (city)") and largest city is [Indianapolis](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis,_Indiana "Indianapolis, Indiana"). Other cities and towns include [Bloomington](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bloomington,_Indiana "Bloomington, Indiana"), [Gary](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Gary,_Indiana "Gary, Indiana"), [Fort Wayne](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fort_Wayne,_Indiana "Fort Wayne, Indiana"), [South Bend](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/South_Bend,_Indiana "South Bend, Indiana"), [Evansville](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Evansville,_Indiana "Evansville, Indiana"), [Muncie](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Muncie,_Indiana "Muncie, Indiana"), [Lafayette](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lafayette,_Indiana "Lafayette, Indiana"), and [Marion](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marion,_Indiana "Marion, Indiana"). People who live in Indiana are sometimes called _Hoosiers_.

Indiana's state bird is the [cardinal](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Northern_cardinal "Northern cardinal"). Indiana's state flower is the [peony](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Peony "Peony"). Indiana's state tree is a tulip tree.

Indiana has [borders](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Border "Border") with four other states: [Illinois](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Illinois "Illinois"), [Kentucky](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky "Kentucky"), [Michigan](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Michigan "Michigan"), and [Ohio](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ohio "Ohio"). It also touches [Lake Michigan](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lake_Michigan "Lake Michigan"), which is the only one of the [Great Lakes](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lakes "Great Lakes") that is completely inside the United States.

## History

[[change](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&veaction=edit&section=1 "Change section: History") | [change source](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&action=edit&section=1 "Edit section's source code: History")]

Before it was called Indiana, most people who lived there were [indigenous](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indigenous_peoples "Indigenous peoples"). [France](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/France "France")[colonized](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Colonized "Colonized") the area, renaming it [New France](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/New_France "New France"). They [enslaved](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Slavery "Slavery") the indigenous peoples, and introduced [black](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Black "Black") slaves.[[6]](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana#cite_note-7) In the [18th century](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/18th_century "18th century"), the United States took the area from France. Many slave owners from [Kentucky](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kentucky "Kentucky") and [Virginia](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Virginia "Virginia") moved into the area.[[7]](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana#cite_note-8) In 1816, it had became the state called Indiana. The government ordered all slaves to be freed, but black people were still enslaved there into the 1830s.[[8]](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana#cite_note-9)

## Attractions

[[change](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&veaction=edit&section=2 "Change section: Attractions") | [change source](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&action=edit&section=2 "Edit section's source code: Attractions")]

One attraction of Indiana is the [Indianapolis Motor Speedway](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Motor_Speedway "Indianapolis Motor Speedway"), where the [Indianapolis 500](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_500 "Indianapolis 500") is held every year. The Indianapolis 500 is one of the biggest car races in the United States. The Indianapolis Motor Speedway is not actually in Indianapolis. It is in a town called [Speedway](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Speedway,_Indiana "Speedway, Indiana"), which is completely surrounded by Indianapolis.

## Sports

[[change](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&veaction=edit&section=3 "Change section: Sports") | [change source](https://simple.wikipedia.org/w/index.php?title=Indiana&action=edit&section=3 "Edit section's source code: Sports")]

The [Indianapolis 500](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_500 "Indianapolis 500") car race is based in Indianapolis. Professional sports teams include the [Indianapolis Colts](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indianapolis_Colts "Indianapolis Colts") of the [National Football League](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Football_League "National Football League") (NFL) and the [Indiana Pacers](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_Pacers "Indiana Pacers") of the [National Basketball Association](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/National_Basketball_Association "National Basketball Association") (NBA). Colleges and universities in Indiana with well-known sports teams include [Purdue University](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Purdue_University "Purdue University"), the [University of Notre Dame](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/University_of_Notre_Dame "University of Notre Dame"), and [Indiana University](https://simple.wikipedia.org/wiki/Indiana_University "Indiana University").
