## Draft Memo Text

**MEMORANDUM**

**TO:** Fleet Management
**FROM:** Fleet Cost Analyst
**DATE:** October 4, 2026
**SUBJECT:** TCO Analysis: Diesel vs. Battery-Electric Class 8 Tractors

**Executive Summary**
This analysis evaluates the 7-year Total Cost of Ownership (TCO) for replacing 20 Class 8 tractors with battery-electric vehicles (BEVs) for regional routes in Indiana. Despite substantial operational savings in energy and maintenance, the high initial purchase price and infrastructure requirements result in a BEV TCO that is approximately $75,000 higher per truck than diesel over a 7-year period ($568,600 vs $493,538).

**TCO Comparison (Per Truck)**
| Component | Diesel | BEV |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Net Purchase Price | $185,000 | $380,000 ($420,000 - $40,000) |
| 7-Year Energy Cost | $245,538 | $100,800 |
| 7-Year Maintenance | $63,000 | $37,800 |
| Infrastructure | $0 | $50,000 |
| **Total TCO** | **$493,538** | **$568,600** |

**Key Drivers**
1. **Capital Expenditure:** The BEV purchase price is the primary cost driver. Even with the $40,000 federal IRA 45W credit, the net capital cost is more than double that of a diesel tractor.
2. **Energy Savings:** BEVs offer a significant operational advantage, reducing energy costs by approximately 59% ($144,738 savings per truck over 7 years) based on Indiana commercial electricity estimates.
3. **Maintenance:** Reduced mechanical complexity in BEVs leads to an estimated 40% reduction in maintenance expenses.
4. **Infrastructure:** The requirement for DC fast charging and grid upgrades adds a significant upfront cost of $50,000 per vehicle.

**Recommendation**
Based on the current financial modeling, **I recommend continuing with Diesel tractors for the 2026 procurement cycle.** 

The BEV option does not achieve TCO parity under current Indiana energy rates and purchase prices. The transition to BEV would only be financially viable if the company secures additional state-level incentives or if diesel prices increase significantly beyond current projections.
