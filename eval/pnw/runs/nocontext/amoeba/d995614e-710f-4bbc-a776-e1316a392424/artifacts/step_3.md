### Fines and Penalties
The following violation fines apply:
* **No Valid Permit:** $25 [S7]
* **Improper Parking:** $10 [S7]
* **Moving Violations:** $25 [S7]
* **Violation of Reserved Space:** $50 [S7]
* **Use of Stolen Permit:** $100 [S7]
* **Violation of Handicapped Space:** $100 [S7]
