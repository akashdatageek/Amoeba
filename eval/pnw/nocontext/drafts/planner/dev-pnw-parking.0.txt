## Thought
The objective is to provide a clear, actionable guide on how to pay a parking citation at Purdue University Northwest (PNW). This requires identifying the official payment portal, alternative payment methods (mail/in-person), the specific data needed to process the payment, and any time-sensitive constraints like deadlines. I will employ a Researcher to extract this data from the official university sources and a Technical Writer to synthesize it into a user-friendly set of instructions.

## Requirements:
R1: Identify all official payment methods for PNW University parking citations.
R2: Provide step-by-step instructions for each identified payment method.
R3: List the specific information (e.g., citation number, license plate) required to make a payment.
R4: Identify payment deadlines and penalties for late payments.

## Givens and Assumptions:
- given: The target institution is Purdue University Northwest (PNW).
- assumption: The university provides a public-facing web portal for citation payments.
- assumption: The user has access to the internet to perform the payment.

## Risks and Decisions:
- **Risk**: The university may have moved its payment system to a third-party vendor whose pages are not easily indexed or require a login to see the "how-to" section.
- **Decision**: If the Researcher cannot find a public "how-to" page, they will look for the "Parking Rules and Regulations" PDF, which typically outlines the legal payment process and deadlines.
- **Risk**: Multiple campuses (West Lake vs. Hammond).
- **Decision**: The Researcher will check if payment processes differ by campus; if they are unified, a single process will be documented. If they differ, the guide will be split by campus.
