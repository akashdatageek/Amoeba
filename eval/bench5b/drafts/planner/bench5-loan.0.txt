## Thought
The task is a straightforward financial calculation requiring the application of the standard amortization formula. To ensure accuracy, I will employ a Financial Analyst to perform the calculation and a QA Reviewer to verify the numbers independently. A Summarizer will then package the formula and the result into the final response. I will use the `calc` tool for basic arithmetic or the `python_interpreter` for higher precision.

## Requirements:
R1: Calculate the monthly payment on a $250,000 equipment loan.
R2: Show the formula used for the calculation.

## Givens and Assumptions:
- given: Principal ($P$) = $250,000
- given: Annual Percentage Rate ($APR$) = 7% (0.07)
- given: Loan Term = 5 years
- given: Payment Frequency = Monthly
- derived: Monthly Interest Rate ($r$) = $0.07 / 12 \approx 0.00583333$
- derived: Total Number of Payments ($n$) = $5 \times 12 = 60$
- assumption: The loan is a standard fixed-rate amortizing loan.
- assumption: Payments are made at the end of each period (ordinary annuity).

- **Risk:** Ambiguity in payment timing (beginning vs. end of month).
- **Decision:** Assumed "ordinary annuity" (payment at the end of the month) as it is the industry standard for equipment loans unless specified otherwise.
