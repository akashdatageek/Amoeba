## Global Brand Style Guide
*   **Visual Theme:** "Executive Minimalist" — High whitespace, sharp edges, high contrast.
*   **Color Palette:**
    *   **Primary:** Deep Navy (`#002060 [unverified]`) — Used for headers, primary shapes, and "On-Time" data.
    *   **Secondary:** Slate Grey (`#7F7F7F [unverified]`) — Used for sub-text and "Late" data.
    *   **Accent:** Vibrant Teal (`#00B0F0 [unverified]`) — Used for callouts, highlights, and action items.
    *   **Background:** Pure White (`#FFFFFF [unverified]`).
*   **Typography:** 
    *   **Headers:** Bold Sans-Serif (e.g., Segoe UI Bold), Navy.
    *   **Body:** Regular Sans-Serif (e.g., Segoe UI), Slate Grey.
*   **Logo Placement:** Top Right corner, small and monochromatic.
*   **Footer:** Page number bottom right; "Confidential - Freight Operations" bottom left in 8pt [unverified] Slate Grey.
