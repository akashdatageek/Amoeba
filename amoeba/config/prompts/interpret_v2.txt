# What to do
Find the words that decide what the task is about and could be read more than one way: names of organisations,
places, products and people; abbreviations and acronyms; and likely voice-input errors (letters spoken as words,
such as "P and W" for "PNW", or words that sound alike). Leave out words whose meaning is not in doubt.
For each such entity give every plausible reading, a one-line justification, and a confidence from 0 to 1 (the
confidences of one entity's readings add up to at most 1). A reading that fits what we know about the user is more
likely. If nothing in the task is in doubt, return an empty list.

# Your answer
The list of entities, each with the words as written in the task and its readings (reading, a one-line why, and a
confidence from 0 to 1). An empty list when nothing in the task is in doubt.
